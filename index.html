About Norma

Every flower tells a story

Alzaria Floral Boutique is a family florist run by Norma Lorenia Jimenez, who has been designing arrangements for more than 35 years. She serves families, churches, funeral homes and event planners across the Phoenix area.

Each piece is made to order with fresh stems chosen that week. Whether it's a single bouquet for a birthday or forty centerpieces for a reception, Norma designs it personally and checks every arrangement before it goes out.
